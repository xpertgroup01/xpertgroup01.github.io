Empire Atlas Uploader is an owner-operated tool. This notice explains how it uses information obtained through Google and YouTube APIs.

Information collected and accessed

With account-owner authorization, the application may access the authorized YouTube channel's ID and video information, OAuth authorization credentials, and approved video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and caption files. It records upload identifiers, results and timestamps to prevent accidental duplicates.

Why the information is used

Information is used only to verify the correct channel, upload approved videos, attach authorized assets such as captions and thumbnails, prevent duplicate uploads, and maintain operational audit records. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Sharing and security

The application sends required information to Google and YouTube to perform authorized operations. Technical providers involved in hosting the owner's workflow may process the limited information necessary for operation. Credentials are intended to be stored in access-restricted secret storage and are not published on this website. YouTube user data is not sold or shared with advertising partners.

Retention and deletion

Credentials and operational records are retained only while necessary for authorized channel operations, security, and duplicate prevention. The account owner can revoke the app's Google access through Google Account settings. For questions, access or deletion requests, contact empireatlasstudio@gmail.com. Some minimal records may be kept when legally required.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This page will be updated when the application's privacy practices materially change.
