Empire Atlas Uploader

Empire Atlas Uploader is the owner-operated utility used to deliver approved historical documentaries and Shorts to the Empire Atlas YouTube channel. It is an internal application, not a public sign-up service.

With the channel owner's Google OAuth authorization, the tool verifies YouTube channel identity and uploads approved videos; it may also attach authorized thumbnails or captions. Content remains private unless release is explicitly approved under the configured publishing policy.
