About the application

Empire Atlas Uploader is an owner-operated application that helps the Empire Atlas historical documentary channel upload approved videos and Shorts to its own YouTube account. This is an internal utility, not a public sign-up service.

What it does

After the channel owner authorizes Google OAuth access, it verifies YouTube channel identity, uploads approved content and, when supported, sets video thumbnails or captions. Uploaded videos are initially PRIVATE unless the owner explicitly authorizes publishing through a matching release policy.
