This Privacy Policy explains how Savanna Diaries YouTube Publisher (“the application”) accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when the channel owner connects a Google account.

Information the application accesses

When authorized by the channel owner, the application uses Google OAuth credentials and YouTube API access needed to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel. The owner or authorized workflow may provide video files, titles, descriptions, tags, privacy settings, scheduled publication details, and upload status information.

How information is used

  • To authorize and identify the connected YouTube account and channel.
  • To transfer owner-approved video files and associated metadata to YouTube.
  • To check upload status, prevent accidental duplicate uploads, and keep operational records.
  • To troubleshoot and secure the publishing workflow.

Storage and security

OAuth refresh credentials are stored in encrypted form in access-restricted application storage. Upload job details and operational logs may be retained by the application infrastructure for status checks, reliability, and duplicate prevention. Video files and metadata are transmitted to Google/YouTube to perform the requested upload. The application uses reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Sharing

Information is shared with Google and YouTube only as needed to authenticate the account and perform YouTube operations requested by the channel owner. The application may rely on infrastructure providers, including Cloudflare, to host the publishing service and process the minimum data needed to operate it. The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Authorization credentials and operational records are retained only while needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. The channel owner can revoke Google account access at any time through Google Account third-party connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access. For a data access or deletion request, contact savannadiariesofficial@gmail.com.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children's privacy

The application is an internal publishing tool for the channel owner and is not designed for children to create accounts or submit personal information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
