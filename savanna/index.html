ABOUT THE CHANNEL

Welcome to Savanna Diaries

Savanna Diaries is a wildlife-focused video channel sharing stories and videos about animals, habitats, and the natural world.

Savanna Diaries YouTube Publisher

Savanna Diaries YouTube Publisher is an owner-operated tool used to transfer approved video files and their publishing details to the Savanna Diaries YouTube channel. It is not a public sign-up service. Uploads are set to private by default; the channel owner reviews and authorizes any public release.
